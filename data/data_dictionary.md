# Synthetic cardiac dataset dictionary

The dataset is generated by `src/generate_data.py` with NumPy random seed 42. All rows are synthetic; no real person or identifiable health record is represented. Implausible values, missing values, and duplicates are included intentionally for the data-cleaning exercise.

| Column                | Type     | Description                                                | Expected values/range                        | General medical reference                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `patient_id`          | text     | Synthetic row identifier; excluded from modeling           | `SYN-0001`, ...                              |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `age`                 | numeric  | Age in years                                               | 18–100 after validation                      |                                                                                                                                     |
| `sex`                 | category | Synthetic sex category                                     | Female, Male                                 |                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `resting_bp`          | numeric  | Resting systolic blood pressure (mmHg)                     | 70–250 after validation                      | **Normal systolic BP: <120 mmHg**. 120–129 is elevated; 130–139 may indicate Stage 1 hypertension; ≥140 may indicate Stage 2 hypertension.                                                                                                                 |
| `cholesterol`         | numeric  | Serum total cholesterol (mg/dL)                            | 80–600 after validation                      | **Around 150 mg/dL is considered an optimal total cholesterol level.** Total cholesterol above 200 mg/dL may be considered high.                                                                                                                           |
| `bmi`                 | numeric  | Body mass index (kg/m²)                                    | 10–60 after validation                       | **18.5–24.9: healthy weight**, <18.5: underweight, 25.0–29.9: overweight, ≥30: obesity.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `fasting_blood_sugar` | integer  | Elevated fasting blood sugar indicator                     | 0 or 1                                       | `0` represents the preferable/non-elevated state. For the underlying glucose measurement, **<100 mg/dL is generally normal**, 100–125 mg/dL indicates impaired fasting glucose, and ≥126 mg/dL may meet a diabetes threshold when clinically confirmed.    |
| `max_heart_rate`      | numeric  | Maximum heart rate (beats/minute)                          | 50–250 after validation                      | Maximum heart rate is **age-dependent**. A commonly used estimate is approximately `220 − age`. During exercise, about 50–70% of estimated maximum is a moderate-intensity target and 70–85% is vigorous intensity.                                        |
| `exercise_angina`     | category | Exercise-induced angina indicator                          | No, Yes                                      | **No** is generally the preferable finding. `Yes` indicates chest discomfort/angina associated with exercise and should not be interpreted as a normal finding.                                                                                            |
| `chest_pain_type`     | category | Synthetic chest-pain group                                 | Typical, Atypical, Non-anginal, Asymptomatic | There is **no universal “healthy” category** for this synthetic feature. `Asymptomatic` means no reported chest-pain symptoms in this encoding; `Typical` is more consistent with classic angina symptoms. These categories alone cannot diagnose disease. |
| `cardiac_risk`        | integer  | **Classification target**: synthetic elevated-risk pattern | 0 = lower pattern, 1 = elevated pattern      | This is **not a clinical measurement or diagnosis**. `0` and `1` are synthetic labels generated only for the machine-learning exercise.                                                                                                                    |

## Important distinction

The **Expected values/range** column represents the ranges accepted during data validation and cleaning. These limits are intentionally broad and **must not be interpreted as medically normal ranges**.

The **General medical reference** column provides simplified adult clinical reference values for educational interpretation only. Individual medical interpretation may depend on age, medical history, medications, measurement conditions, and other factors.

`cardiac_risk` is a simulated educational label, not a diagnosis. It must never be used to guide treatment or emergency decisions.
